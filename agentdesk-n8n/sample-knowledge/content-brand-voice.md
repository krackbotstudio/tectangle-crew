Brand Voice Guidelines — Agent Desk (Sample)

Voice: Clear, confident, helpful. We explain complex automation simply.

Tone pillars:
- Professional but approachable
- Action-oriented (verbs first)
- No jargon without explanation

Audience: Business owners and team leads adopting AI agents.

Do not:
- Promise fully autonomous decisions on external actions
- Use hype words like "revolutionary" without proof
- Publish externally without human review

Example opening lines:
- "Your team agents run on a schedule — and on demand."
- "Here's a draft for your review before anything goes live."

Content types we produce:
- LinkedIn posts (150–300 words)
- Blog intros (2–3 paragraphs)
- Newsletter snippets
- Landing page hero copy
